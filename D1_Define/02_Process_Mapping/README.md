# 🔄 Process Mapping: SIPOC Analysis
**Project:** Madar-Retail Improvement | **Date:** 06/05/2026

## 📌 Overview
The SIPOC (Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers) diagrams below outline the high-level workflows for both internal management requests and the external customer journey. These maps help identify where engagement gaps occur during the retail experience.

---

## 1. Management Request Process (Internal)
This process covers how products and needs are identified and communicated internally to ensure staff and inventory readiness.

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Suppliers** | Store Manager, Product Supplier, Storage Manager |
| **Inputs** | Needs, Products, Stock Status |
| **Process** | 1. Store manager asks for a product <br> 2. Searching for a specific product <br> 3. Customer waits <br> 4. Customer didn't find staff or meet staff but doesn't meet their needs <br> 5. Customer leaves |
| **Outputs** | More interaction, Staff availability |
| **Customers** | Staff, Customers |

---

## 2. Customer Interaction Process (External)
This process highlights the critical "moment of truth" when a customer enters the store and seeks assistance.

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Suppliers** | Customer, Staff |
| **Inputs** | Needs, Information |
| **Process** | 1. Customer enters store <br> 2. Customer looking for a staff <br> 3. Customer waits <br> 4. Customer didn't find staff or meet staff but doesn't meet their needs <br> 5. Customer leaves |
| **Outputs** | More interaction, Staff availability |
| **Customers** | Staff, Customers |

---

## 💡 Key Findings from SIPOC
*   **Bottleneck:** In both processes, "Customer waits" and "Customer didn't find staff" lead to the same negative outcome: **Customer leaves**.
*   **Gap identified:** There is a clear failure in the "Process" stage to convert "Needs" into a "Sale" due to staff unavailability or lack of engagement.
*   **Focus Area:** The "Improve" phase will target steps 2, 3, and 4 of these processes to ensure staff are proactive rather than reactive.
