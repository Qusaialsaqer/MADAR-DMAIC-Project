
## 1. Project Charter
### **Project Title:** Madar-Retail

### **Business Case**
The Madar-Retail store faces a critical conversion gap where only ~22% of visitors make a purchase. At Sigma 0.7 (DPMO: 780,000), 78% of sales opportunities are lost. Root cause analysis confirmed lack of standardized staff training as the primary driver; staff engagement directly drives sales, while inaction compounds revenue loss.

### **Goal Statement (SMART)**
*   **Target:** Increase store conversion Sigma level from **0.7 to 1.25** within 6 months.
*   **Method:** Implementing a Standardized Staff Training Program.
*   **KPIs:** Reducing DPMO from 780,000 to below 500,000 and improving staff engagement rate to ≥ 90%.

### **Scope Control**
| **In-Scope** | **Out-of-Scope** |
| :--- | :--- |
| Staff training program design & rollout | IT systems & e-commerce platform |
| Conversion rate analysis (1,000 records) | Pricing structure changes |
| Customer engagement process | Store layout redesign |
| Stockout tracking & reporting | HR recruitment & hiring |

---

## 2. Problem Statement (Data-Driven)

### **Problem Description**
In a retail store environment with 1,000 customer interactions analyzed, the store conversion rate is critically low at Sigma 0.7. Customers leave without purchasing due to poor staff engagement, unanswered product questions, and long wait times. Additionally, 38% of customers requested specific SKUs that were out of stock.

### **Baseline vs. Target Metrics**
*   **Current Performance (Sigma 0.7):** DPMO: 780,000 | Conversion Rate: ~22% | Staff Engagement: < 40% | Stockout Rate: ~38%.
*   **Target Performance (Sigma 1.25):** DPMO: < 500,000 | Conversion Rate: ≥ 35% | Staff Engagement: ≥ 90% | Stockout Rate: ≤ 5%.

### **Impact Analysis**
*   **Financial Impact:** Estimated loss of 78% of potential sales transactions. Repeated non-conversion leads to permanent customer churn to online channels.
*   **Operational Impact:** Wasted staff idle time, customer flow imbalance, and long-term brand damage.
