# 🎯 Phase 1: Define - Project Foundation

## 📑 Executive Summary
The **Define** phase serves as the strategic bedrock for the retail performance project. Our primary objective is to identify and quantify the barriers affecting the **Visitor-to-Buyer Conversion Rate**. By synthesizing Stakeholder expectations and the Voice of the Customer (VOC), we have established a clear, data-driven roadmap to optimize store operations and enhance the customer journey.

---

## 📂 Phase Deliverables & Structure
Based on the project repository structure (image_68cdd8.png), this phase is organized into the following modules:

### **01. Project Charter**
Defines the mission, scope, and high-level goals. It establishes the business case for improving conversion rates and sets the initial timeline for the DMAIC cycle.

### **02. Process Mapping**
Provides a high-level visualization of the current customer journey, identifying potential "friction points" from the moment a visitor enters the store until they complete a transaction.

### **03. Voice of the Customer (VOC) & CTQ**
Captures qualitative feedback and translates it into measurable **Critical to Quality (CTQ)** metrics:
*   **Target Conversion Rate:** $\geq 35\%$.
*   **Target Wait Time:** $\leq 3$ minutes (addressing the current 8-minute bottleneck).

### **04. Stakeholder Analysis**
A comprehensive mapping of 6 key stakeholder groups (Sales, Management, Inventory, Finance, Customers, and Vendors):
*   **High Influence:** Store Managers and Sales Associates are crucial for execution.
*   **Risk Management:** Finance and Supply Chain are monitored to prevent budget and stock issues.

### **05. Communications & Data Strategy**
Formal archives of critical project correspondence aimed at closing information gaps:
*   **Operational Request:** Formal email sent to **Meshari Alharbi** requesting 6 data points (Inventory, Density, Timestamps) to isolate technical vs. human performance issues.
*   **Human Capital Request:** Formal email requesting training records for **Reps (A, B, C, D, E)** to correlate skill sets with conversion outcomes.

---

## 📈 Strategic Rationale
This phase ensures that our subsequent **Analyze** phase is built on a foundation of "True Needs" rather than assumptions. By identifying that the **8-minute checkout average** is a significant pain point, we have already prioritized technical and operational bottlenecks for investigation.
