# 🗣️ Voice of the Customer (VOC) & CTQ Tree Analysis
**Project:** Madar-Retail Improvement | **Date:** 06/05/2026

## 📌 Overview
This section focuses on capturing the **Voice of the Customer (VOC)** from both direct and indirect sources to identify pain points. These qualitative insights are then translated into **Critical to Quality (CTQ)** requirements with specific metrics and targets.

---

## 1. VOC Analysis (Direct & Indirect)
We categorized customer feedback to understand the "Why" behind low conversion rates.

| Source | Raw VOC (Customer Feedback) | Identified Root Cause |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct** | "No one approached me," "Staff didn't understand my needs," "Accessories not offered." | Lack of Training |
| **Direct** | "Waited too long at checkout," "Store busy but service slow." | Lack of Staff |
| **Indirect** | "Enter store and leave within minutes without interaction." | Lack of Training |
| **Indirect** | "Check prices on phones," "Purchase online after store visit." | No Advantage (Price/Value) |
| **Indirect** | "Spend long time at demo tables but don't convert." | Problem with the Demo |

---

## 2. VOC to CTQ Tree (Translation to Metrics)
To solve the issues identified above, we translated customer "Needs" into measurable "CTQs".

### **Key Performance Targets**

| Customer VOC | Customer NEED | Critical to Quality (CTQ) | Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Staff didn't understand my needs" | Knowledgeable & proactive staff | **Staff Engagement Rate** | % Customers greeted within 2 min | **≥ 90%** |
| "Leave if not engaged within 3-5 min" | Adequate floor staffing | **Floor Staff Availability** | Staff-to-customer ratio | **≥ 1:5** |
| "I waited too long" | Fast & efficient service | **Service Response Time** | Average wait time (min) | **≤ 3 min** |
| "Couldn't find the product" | Product availability | **Stock Availability Rate** | % of SKUs in stock | **≥ 95%** |
| "Buy only when bundles explained" | Trained staff who explain value | **Conversion Rate** | % visitors who purchase | **≥ 35%** |
| "Found it cheaper online" | Competitive in-store pricing | **Price Competitiveness** | Price gap vs online | **≤ 5%** |

---

## 📈 Strategic Insights
*   **Engagement is Priority #1:** Translating the VOC "No one approached me" into a **90% Greeting Target** is the most critical step to moving the Sigma level from 0.7 to 1.25.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Reducing wait times to **under 3 minutes** and maintaining a **1:5 staff ratio** directly addresses the "Service is slow" feedback.
*   **Value Proposition:** By ensuring price gaps are **≤ 5%**, we mitigate the "Channel Shift" where customers browse in-store but buy online.
