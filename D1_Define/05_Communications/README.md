## 📊 Data Acquisition Summary: Closing the Information Gaps

To move from the **Define** phase to a high-precision **Analyze** phase, we identified critical gaps in the initial dataset. Two formal data requests were initiated to capture missing operational and human capital variables.

### **1. Operational & Customer Friction Data**
*   **Focus:** Inventory, Transaction Value, and Queue Bottlenecks.
*   **Key Discovery:** The current **9.62-minute checkout average** was identified as a primary suspect for cart abandonment.
*   **Objective:** To determine if low conversion is driven by **Technical/Operational friction** (long lines, out-of-stocks) rather than just staff performance.

### **2. Human Capital & Training Maturity**
*   **Focus:** Training records and joining dates for specific Sales Representatives (A, B, C, D, E).
*   **Objective:** To correlate **Staff Tenure and Training Levels** with conversion success. 
*   **Hypothesis:** Gaps in "Upselling" and "Product Knowledge" modules may be the root cause of missed accessory sales and poor customer engagement.

### **Strategic Rationale**
By securing these 7 additional data points (6 operational + 1 training), the project ensures that the **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)** is:
*   **Evidence-Based:** Moving beyond assumptions about staff behavior.
*   **Holistic:** Covering the entire customer journey from store entry to final checkout.
*   **Actionable:** Identifying whether the solution should be a **System Fix** (Better checkout/Inventory) or a **People Fix** (Targeted training).
